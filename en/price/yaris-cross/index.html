Toyota Yaris Cross questions

How much is the Toyota Yaris Cross 2026?

The Toyota Yaris Cross on-the-road price is RM99,900 to RM109,900 (Peninsular Malaysia, before rebate, including SST, excluding insurance), checked 25 November 2026.

What is the monthly instalment for a Toyota Yaris Cross?

Estimates start at about RM970 a month for the Yaris Cross 1.5S, with a 10% deposit over 9 years at 2.30% a year, after rebate (if any). Insurance is not included. The actual rate and approval depend on the bank.

Is there a rebate on the Toyota Yaris Cross this month?

Yes. The November 2026 rebate on the Toyota Yaris Cross is up to RM3,500, depending on the variant and subject to terms and conditions.
