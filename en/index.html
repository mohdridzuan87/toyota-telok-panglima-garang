    FAQ

    GOT QUESTIONS? HERE ARE THE ANSWERS.

    Do the Toyota prices on this site include insurance?

    No. Prices are official Toyota Malaysia OTR prices for Peninsular Malaysia (Individual Private), including SST but without insurance and without rebate. The calculator above gives a separate first-year insurance estimate.

    How much deposit do I need to buy a Toyota?

    It depends on bank approval and your profile. Many buyers choose around 10%. Send your calculation to Wan Jasni, and Wan Jasni will check the most comfortable option for you.

    What is the longest car loan tenure?

    Up to 9 years, subject to bank approval. Car loans in Malaysia use a flat interest rate, so total interest is calculated on the original loan amount for the whole tenure.

    What warranty comes with a new Toyota?

    New Toyota passenger cars come with a 5-year unlimited mileage warranty. The Hilux and Hiace have 5 years or 150,000 km, whichever comes first. The warranty stays valid as long as service and inspections follow the schedule.

    Is there a rebate or promotion this month?

    Toyota Malaysia runs new promotions almost every month, and the value differs by model and variant. WhatsApp Wan Jasni for the current rebate on the model you like, subject to terms and conditions.

    How long does the car take to arrive after booking?

    It depends on the model, variant and colour. Some models are in stock, others need a wait. Wan Jasni checks current stock before you book.

    I live in Banting, Klang or Shah Alam. Can I buy from Wan Jasni?

    Yes. The Telok Panglima Garang showroom is close to Kuala Langat, Banting, Jenjarom, Klang and Shah Alam. Much of the early work, such as loan calculations and document checks, can be done over WhatsApp first.
